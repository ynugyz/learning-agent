# Human Note Quality v1

Evaluate the candidate note as a learning asset, not as a transcript summary.
Judge coverage, conceptual sufficiency, examples/formulas/code/process,
boundaries and epistemic wording, preservation of existing content, noise and
duplication, relation/link utility and review usability as independent
dimensions. Return structured decisions only. Identify a target section and a
local repair when a gap is clear; do not rewrite the whole note and do not add
knowledge that is absent from the supplied evidence.
